1What is CapCut?

CapCut is ByteDance's video editing app, available on mobile (iOS/Android), desktop (Windows/macOS), and as a browser-based web editor at capcut.com. It started as a lightweight mobile trimmer built to pair naturally with TikTok, and has since grown into a genuinely full-featured editor: multi-track timelines, keyframe animation, AI-assisted tools, and a large library of templates, transitions, and effects.

Its core appeal hasn't changed — a clean interface that doesn't require prior editing experience, combined with AI tools that used to require real skill or dedicated software (background removal, auto-captioning, noise reduction) now sitting one tap away.

Download safely. The only reliable source for CapCut is the official app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store) or capcut.com directly. Third-party "modded" or "unlocked" APK sites frequently bundle adware or malware disguised as a free upgrade — there's no safe shortcut around the official subscription for paid features.

2The free plan, in detail

CapCut's free tier remains one of the most generous in mobile video editing, and for a lot of casual creators, it's genuinely all they'll ever need.

  • Multi-track timeline editing with cut, split, and trim tools
  • Keyframe animation and speed ramping
  • Chroma key (green screen) and basic stabilization
  • A large library of free music, sound effects, filters, and effects
  • Basic AI voiceover with text-to-speech
  • 1080p export

The catch: since the 2025 restructure, more templates and effects are marked "Pro-only" than before, and using a Pro-tagged asset on the free plan exports with a watermark.

3Standard tier explained

Standard sits between free and Pro, and is aimed primarily at mobile users who just want the watermark gone.

  • Removes the watermark from Pro-tagged templates and effects
  • Unlocks additional templates, transitions, and text styles
  • Sold mainly through app stores, monthly billing only in most regions

What it doesn't include: 4K export, the full AI toolkit, or the larger cloud storage allowance — those are Pro-only.

4Pro tier explained

capcut pro is where CapCut's full toolkit actually lives. Since the restructure, this is the tier that includes the AI-heavy features, not Standard — a common point of confusion, since the old "Pro" name used to cover what's now split across two tiers.

  • 4K export (up from the 1080p cap on Free and Standard)
  • The complete AI toolkit — see the breakdown below
  • Roughly 1TB of cloud storage for syncing projects across devices
  • Full premium template, transition, font, and music library, with commercial licensing
  • Faster export processing on longer or higher-resolution projects

8Is upgrading worth it?

The honest answer: it depends entirely on whether you actually hit the free plan's limits, not on the plan's name or marketing.

Stay on Free if:

You're editing casually, exporting at 1080p is fine, and you don't mind CapCut's own watermark on premium-tagged assets.

Standard makes sense if:

You just want the watermark gone and don't need 4K or the AI toolkit — common for mobile-only creators posting straight to TikTok/Reels.

Pro makes sense if:

You're producing regularly, need 4K for client work, rely on the AI tools weekly, or need commercial licensing for brand/client projects. At that point the time saved on tasks like auto-captioning or background removal usually outweighs the subscription cost.

A simple test that works well in practice: run one real project on the free plan first. Note exactly where you hit a wall — a watermark you can't remove, a resolution cap, an AI feature that's locked. If that same wall keeps showing up project after project, that's the actual signal to upgrade — not the plan name.

9Tips before you subscribe

  • Compare checkout channels. Web checkout via capcut.com is often meaningfully cheaper than subscribing through the iOS or Android app store, since app stores add their own commission.
  • Annual billing saves the most. Pro's annual plan works out to roughly $15/month effective, versus $19.99 billed monthly.
  • Cancel through the channel you subscribed on. If you subscribed via the App Store or Play Store, you have to cancel there — cancelling inside CapCut's own app won't stop the billing.
  • Check for a free trial. A 7-day trial is available in some regions — worth testing the AI toolkit properly before committing to a year.

10Frequently asked questions

What's the actual difference between Standard and Pro?
Standard mainly removes the watermark and unlocks more templates. Pro adds 4K export, the full AI toolkit, much larger cloud storage, and commercial licensing — the AI features specifically only live in Pro.
Can I edit and export without ever paying?
Yes. The free plan covers a complete basic editing workflow at 1080p. You'll only see a watermark if you use a template or effect specifically tagged as Pro.
Why did CapCut's pricing change so much recently?
CapCut restructured its subscriptions between late 2025 and early 2026, splitting the old single paid tier into Standard and Pro, and roughly doubling the top-tier price in the process. If you last checked the price a year or more ago, it's changed.
Is there a lifetime or one-time-purchase option?
No — both Standard and Pro are subscription-only, billed monthly or annually. There's no current one-time-purchase plan.
Does the price change by country?
Yes. CapCut uses regional pricing, so users in different markets can pay noticeably different amounts for the same plan. The renewal total shown on your own signed-in checkout is the number that actually applies to you.
